Engineers with AI is a free site that teaches engineering students to use AI well in their own field. It is for students in aerospace, mechanical, civil, electrical, chemical, manufacturing, and other engineering disciplines outside software.

It has three parts:

- The [Starter Kit](/starter-kit/): Claude Project templates and worked examples for using AI in your courses.
- The [course](/course/), in development: it will take engineering students from no experience with AI to industry-ready in their own field.
- The [community](/community/): discussion boards for sharing work, asking questions, and posting engineering problems that AI might help solve.

## Free and open source

Everything on the site is free, and you don't need an account to read it. The source for the site and the Starter Kit is on GitHub in the [EngineersWithAI](https://github.com/EngineersWithAI) organization. The Starter Kit is released under the MIT License.

## Where it started

Engineers with AI started at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC), and an [in-person community](/community/#at-uiuc) is starting there.

## Contribute {#contribute}

- **Report an error or suggest a change.** Open an issue in the relevant repository on [GitHub](https://github.com/EngineersWithAI), or ask on the [Questions](/community/#questions) board.
- **Propose a fix.** Send a pull request. Factual claims need a link to a primary source.
- **Share your work.** Post what you build, and what you learned from it, on the [community boards](/community/).
